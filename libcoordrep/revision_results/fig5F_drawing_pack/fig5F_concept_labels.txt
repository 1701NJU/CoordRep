── Caption Wording ──

"Hard-control retrieval benchmark. Candidate pools share the same metal
element(s); only CoordRep higher-order fields (donor environment, bridge
topology, mu-pattern) can discriminate true homologs from hard negatives.
CoordRep full achieves 86.7% top-1 correct vs 67.8% for donor-only
baseline (+18.9 pp). Manual chemical audit confirms 93.3% of CoordRep
top-1 matches are chemically homologous, vs 56.7% for donor baseline."
