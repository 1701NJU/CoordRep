Example 4. Molecular multinuclear record graph

Refcode: ETOREN
Chemistry: Cu₂(μ-Cl)₂Cl₂(PR₃)₂ — di-μ-chloro bridged dicopper(I)
Formula: C36 H48 Cl4 Cu2 N4 P2

{GraphType:molecular_multinuclear|nM:2}

M1=[Metal:Cu|ox:unk|CN_site:4|CN_atom:4]
  LocalSphere:M1{S1,S2,S3,S5}

M2=[Metal:Cu|ox:unk|CN_site:4|CN_atom:4]
  LocalSphere:M2{S1,S3,S4,S6}

MetalGraph:
  {M1--M2|relation:bridged|MM_bond:no}

Bridges:
  {site:S1|ligand:L1|donor:Cl(Cl1)|targets:M1,M2|mu:2|mode:mu2-Cl}
  {site:S3|ligand:L3|donor:Cl(Cl3)|targets:M1,M2|mu:2|mode:mu2-Cl}

Terminal sites:
  S2={L2:Cl(Cl2)->M1}
  S4={L4:Cl(Cl4)->M2}
  S5={L5:P(P1)->M1}
  S6={L6:P(P2)->M2}

Ligands:
  L1=[Cl] (bridging)
  L2=[Cl] (terminal, M1)
  L3=[Cl] (bridging)
  L4=[Cl] (terminal, M2)
  L5=[P]  (terminal, M1)
  L6=[P]  (terminal, M2)
