Figure 4. Compositional records distinguish coordination states beyond molecular connectivity. (A) Idealized two-dimensional heavy-atom drawings of trans-bis(β-alaninato)platinum(II) (SUHQOC) and cis-bis(β-alaninato)platinum(II) (SUHQIW). Both contain two N,O-chelating β-alaninato ligands and the same atom- and bond-attributed graph, but SUHQOC has opposed N–N and O–O donor pairs, whereas SUHQIW has two opposed N–O pairs; their computed tmQM dipole moments are 0.0024 and 12.9543 D, respectively. (B) Among 31 relation-resolved cis/trans pairs, 30 have a larger computed dipole for the cis member; lines connect members of the same attributed-graph pair. (C) On the frozen 34-pair cis/trans cohort selected without target information, restoring donor relations lowers the matched CoordRep-hybrid dipole pair-difference MAE from 5.218 to 1.328 D (74.6%) and raises the direction-concordance point estimate from 79.4% to 94.1%. GINE, SchNet, and ViSNet give MAEs of 5.869, 0.800, and 0.481 D, respectively. (D) In a target-conditioned descriptive stress test of 44 graph-identical pairs with different best-shape assignments and |ΔEgap| > 0.10 eV, restoring shape/CShM lowers the matched CoordRep-hybrid MAE from 0.360 to 0.245 eV (31.8%) and raises the direction-concordance point estimate from 67.0% to 84.1%; GINE, SchNet, and ViSNet give MAEs of 0.536, 0.375, and 0.391 eV, respectively. Error bars are pointwise 95% attributed-graph-cluster bootstrap intervals for the frozen three-seed ensembles; intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for multiplicity. Panel D tests shape-field sensitivity within the selected pairs and is not an unbiased generalization benchmark. Only each full-versus-field-hidden CoordRep comparison is an equal-architecture ablation; graph and Cartesian models are unequal-information comparators.
