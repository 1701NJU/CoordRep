## Frozen property-benchmark boundary

The active Figure 4 is intentionally retained unchanged from the property
benchmark reported in the manuscript. The relation-field full/masked hybrids
use frozen CoordRep 1.1.2rc2 inputs, whereas the shape/CShM full/masked hybrids
use frozen CoordRep 1.1.2rc1 inputs. GINE, SchNet, and ViSNet use their frozen
graph or Cartesian inputs. No Figure 4 result is relabelled as an rc3 model
result, and no claim is made that these property models were retrained after
the canonicalizer update. Figure 2 supplies the rc3 canonicalization evidence;
Figure 6A–C supplies the separate rc3 molecular-identity revalidation.

Earlier Figure 4 drafts are retained under `archive/legacy_figure4/` and are
marked historical/superseded in the public artifact manifest.
