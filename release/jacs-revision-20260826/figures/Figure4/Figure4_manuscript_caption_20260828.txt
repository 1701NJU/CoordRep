Figure 4. Explicit CoordRep fields distinguish graph-degenerate coordination states and recover their computed-property ordering. (A) The isomeric pair trans-[Pt(β-Ala)2] (CSD refcode: SUHQOC) and cis-[Pt(β-Ala)2] (CSD refcode: SUHQIW) possesses identical attributed two-dimensional graph topology, yet exhibits divergent trans-donor pairings and computed dipole moments. (B) Among 31 relation-resolved cis/trans pairs, 30 have a larger computed dipole for the cis member; lines connect paired tmQM targets. (C) On the frozen 34-pair cis/trans cohort selected without target information, restoring donor relations lowers the matched CoordRep-hybrid dipole pair-difference MAE from 5.218 to 1.328 D and raises the direction-concordance point estimate from 79.4% to 94.1%. GINE, SchNet, and ViSNet give MAEs of 5.869, 0.800, and 0.481 D, respectively. (D) In a target-conditioned descriptive stress test of 44 graph-identical, different-shape pairs selected by |ΔEgap| > 0.10 eV, restoring shape/CShM lowers the matched CoordRep-hybrid MAE from 0.360 to 0.245 eV and raises the direction-concordance point estimate from 67.0% to 84.1%; GINE, SchNet, and ViSNet give 0.536, 0.375, and 0.391 eV. Error bars are pointwise 95% attributed-graph-cluster bootstrap intervals for frozen three-seed ensembles. Panel D is a target-conditioned descriptive field-sensitivity analysis rather than an unbiased generalization benchmark.
