Figure 3. Continuous shape coordinates resolve local coordination geometry without forcing a single discrete class. (A–C) Pairwise CShM coordinates for the four-, five-, and six-coordinate subsets of a fixed 48,057-record tmQM/GFN2-xTB cohort, computed using exact coordinate-space donor-to-reference assignments. Hexagons show record density on a common logarithmic color scale, and the dashed diagonal marks equal CShM scores for the two implemented reference polyhedra. Counts report nearest-reference assignments: CN = 4, 13,011 SP and 3,671 Td records (N = 16,682); CN = 5, 5,319 SPY and 1,037 TBP records (N = 6,356); and CN = 6, 5,580 Oh and 5,245 TPr records (N = 10,825). (D) Records near a shape boundary, defined before rounding as ΔS = S(second) − S(best) < 1. The boundary descriptor identifies 39/16,682 CN = 4 records (0.23%), 693/6,356 CN = 5 records (10.90%), and 294/10,825 CN = 6 records (2.72%), or 1,026/33,863 records overall (3.03%). These coordinates quantify proximity to idealized reference shapes in static structural snapshots. (E) Two Zn-centered CN = 5 first coordination spheres in the tmQM/GFN2-xTB cohort, with N4S and N2O3 donor sets (CSD refcodes ABAJET and ABOXOI, respectively), both retain SPY as the nearest reference. The spheres are rendered from the same coordinate payloads and donor selections used for the CShM calculation. In the fixed CN = 5 CoordRep order VCN5 = [S(TBP), S(SPY)], their vectors are [4.478, 1.304] and [3.887, 3.342], with ΔS = 3.174 and 0.545, respectively; only the N2O3 environment satisfies ΔS < 1. The fifth Zn–O contact in the N2O3 environment is 2.398 Å, whereas the noncoordinating O atom at 3.110 Å is excluded.
