Figure 3. Continuous shape coordinates resolve local coordination geometry without forcing a single discrete class. (A-C) Pairwise coordinate-space continuous shape measures (CShMs) for the four-, five-, and six-coordinate subsets of a fixed 48,057-record tmQM/GFN2-xTB cohort. Hexagons show record density on a common logarithmic color scale, and the dashed diagonal marks equal CShM scores for the two implemented reference polyhedra. The labels report nearest-reference assignments: CN = 4, 13,011 SP and 3,671 Td records (N = 16,682); CN = 5, 5,319 SPY and 1,037 TBP records (N = 6,356); and CN = 6, 5,580 Oh and 5,245 TPr records (N = 10,825). (D) Records near a numerical shape boundary, defined from unrounded values as Delta S = S(second) - S(best) < 1. The boundary descriptor identifies 39/16,682 CN = 4 records (0.23%), 693/6,356 CN = 5 records (10.90%), and 294/10,825 CN = 6 records (2.72%), or 1,026/33,863 records overall (3.03%). (E) ABAJET and ABOXOI are Zn-centered CN = 5 tmQM/GFN2-xTB snapshots with SPY as the same nearest reference. Their observed first coordination spheres are rendered from the same coordinate payloads used for the CShM calculation and contain the five donors selected by the implemented donor definition: N4S and N2O3, respectively. The fifth ABOXOI contact is Zn-O = 2.398 Å; the noncoordinating O atom at 3.110 Å is excluded. In the frozen CN = 5 serialization order V_CN5 = [S(TBP), S(SPY)], their vectors are [4.478, 1.304] and [3.887, 3.342], respectively. Their unrounded margins are 3.174 and 0.545; only ABOXOI satisfies Delta S < 1. These coordinates describe static proximity to idealized references and do not by themselves establish a reaction pathway or dynamic interconversion. The two snapshots illustrate information retained beyond a shared discrete label and are not a matched-composition control.
