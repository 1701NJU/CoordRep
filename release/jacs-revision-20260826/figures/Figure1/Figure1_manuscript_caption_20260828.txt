Figure 1. Canonical, continuous, and compositional representation of coordination structure. (A) Chemical anatomy of a CoordRep-State. Deconstruction of the experimental cisplatin coordination sphere (CSD refcode: CUKRAB02) into four modular descriptors: metal electronic context, continuous shape metrics, donor-pair spatial relations (2 trans, 4 cis), and ligand identities. (B) Canonical representation. Equivalent 3D inputs related by arbitrary coordinate rotation or atom reindexing converge to a unique, deterministically ordered state (D1–D4) via intrinsic distance matrices. (C) Continuous shape metrics. CShM values evaluated using CoSyMLib quantify geometric distortion against ideal polyhedral references, unambiguously classifying the distorted square-planar state [S(SP-4) = 0.304 vs S(T-4) = 33.287]. (D) Compositional syntax decomposes the state into typed, independently accessible fields (Metal → Shape → Relations → Ligands), enabling exact validation and lossless reserialization.
