Figure 6. Multi-resolution coordination identity and periodic stereochemical resolution. (A) Schematic of the nested molecular hierarchy (L0–L3); filled circles denote fields retained at each level. (B) Pairwise within-family agreement after rc3 re-encoding of the unchanged frozen cohort of 3,491 CSD multi-record refcode families (9,056 records). (C) Polymorphism within a [CuCl4]2− coordination family represented by five structures in the XEYVEC base-refcode family. The yellow C2/c group is illustrated by XEYVEC, whereas the green P-1 group is illustrated by XEYVEC01. Structures are shown aligned to a common square-planar reference, demonstrating how distinct L0–L2 states converge to a single L3 family. (D) Resolution of orientation parity illustrated with an enantiomorphic [Zn(N)(O)(S)(Cl)] pair. Identical Gram matrices yield one MID, whereas the opposite illustrated parity signs, χ123 < 0 and χ123 > 0, separate them into two SIDs. (E) Distribution of MID-to-SID splitting across the CSD MOF Collection and its correspondence with crystallographic space-group symmetry (non-Sohncke versus Sohncke).
