Figure 6. Multi-resolution coordination identity connects molecular families to orientation-sensitive periodic local states. (A) CoordRep-ID defines four nested molecular comparison levels; filled circles identify the fields retained at each level. L0 is the finest canonical geometry-resolved record at the stated serialization precision; L1 retains the binned-shape and donor-relation signatures together with oxidation state, CN, metal, and ligand identities; L2 retains the best-shape label, metal, CN, and ligand identities; and L3 provides a metal/CN/ligand-payload family key, formally reported as ConnID when ligand connectivity is supported by SMILES-resolved provenance. (B) The frozen rc2 selection contained 101,878 strict molecular records and 84,453 connectivity-supported records. An end-to-end April 2025 CSD rerun with CoordRep 1.1.2rc3 yielded 101,794 and 84,449 records, respectively. The active family cohort itself was unchanged: the same 3,491 base-refcode families and 9,056 records were re-encoded under rc3, with no missing L0–L3 key. Mean pairwise within-family agreement is 11.3%, 83.5%, 96.6%, and 97.3% at L0–L3; complete concordance occurs for 357, 2,836, 3,350, and 3,378 families, respectively. (C) The five crystallographic determinations of bis(4-dimethylaminopyridinium) tetrachlorocuprate(II) form two polymorph-associated regions of the CN = 4 CShM plane: the yellow C2/c form (XEYVEC, XEYVEC02, and XEYVEC04; illustrated by XEYVEC) and the green P-1 form (XEYVEC01 and XEYVEC03; illustrated by XEYVEC01). The CuCl4 first-sphere renderings use deposited coordinates after proper-rotation alignment to one common square-planar reference for display. Although the five records remain distinct at L0 and the two regions remain distinct at L1 and L2, all map to one L3 coordination family. (D) CoordRep makes the comparison resolution explicit for periodic local states. A synthetic typed Zn(N)(O)(S)(Cl) first sphere and its exact reflected partner are assigned the same canonical donor order; all 24 input donor permutations of each frame recover the same canonical order and identity keys for that frame. Their metal/site signatures and donor-vector Gram matrices Gij = vi·vj are identical, so CoordRep assigns one MID. Appending the canonical parity-sign tuple χijk = sgn[(vi × vj)·vk] yields opposite signs and therefore two SIDs. Thus, the same CoordRep record schema can retrieve the pair as one metric-equivalent class or retain two orientation-resolved states, according to the chemical comparison being made. Orientation denotes local donor-frame parity rather than direction relative to the unit cell. (E) In the CSD MOF Collection, 23,587 of 36,552 MIDs (64.53%) split into exactly two SIDs, yielding 60,139 SIDs. No CN = 2 MID splits; 60.3–79.9% split at CN = 3–6. Within-entry MID-to-two-SID pairing occurs in 7,721 of 9,189 entries not flagged as Sohncke (84.02%) but in 0 of 1,759 entries flagged as Sohncke. The identities were derived from the supplied P1 coordinate records before the original-space-group flag was joined. Across an independent canonical-invariance audit, equivalent re-expression preserved 400/400 entry-state multisets and all MID/SID assignments at 5,920/5,920 sites under positive-determinant basis changes; MID remained unchanged in 50/50 negative-determinant basis tests. This association does not by itself assign continuous chirality magnitude or macroscopic framework chirality.
