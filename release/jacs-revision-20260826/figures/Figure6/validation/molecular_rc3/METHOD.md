# Figure 6 molecular rc3 rerun

The April 2025 CSD was rescanned entry by entry with the frozen strict molecular
protocol and CoordRep 1.1.2rc3. The high-confidence molecular tier requires one
transition-metal center, atom-resolved eta-1 coordination, a nonpolymeric and
nondisordered three-dimensional source record, coordination number 2-6,
agreement among source, encoded, and serialized coordination counts, a complete
coordinate-space CShM field, strict grammar validation, and bond-order-supported
SMILES payloads for every ligand.

For every CSD refcode family represented by at least two records in that tier,
pairwise agreement was calculated independently at L0-L3 and then averaged
equally across families. A complete family is one for which every within-family
pair agrees at that level. The report compares these rc3 aggregates with the
frozen rc2 scan and audits tier membership and identity hashes. Row-level CSD
derivatives remain private and are not part of the public artifact.
