Figure 3. Continuous shape coordinates resolve local coordination geometry without forcing a single discrete class. (A–C) Pairwise coordinate-space continuous shape measures (CShMs) for the four-, five-, and six-coordinate subsets of a fixed 48,057-record tmQM/GFN2-xTB cohort. Hexagons show record density on a common logarithmic color scale, and the dashed diagonal marks equal CShM scores for the two implemented reference polyhedra. The labels report nearest-reference assignments: CN4, 13,011 SP and 3,671 Td records (N = 16,682); CN5, 5,319 SPY and 1,037 TBP records (N = 6,356); and CN6, 5,580 Oh and 5,245 TPr records (N = 10,825). (D) Records near a numerical shape boundary, defined before rounding as ΔS = S(second) − S(best) < 1. The boundary descriptor identifies 39/16,682 CN4 records (0.23%), 693/6,356 CN5 records (10.90%), and 294/10,825 CN6 records (2.72%), or 1,026/33,863 records overall (3.03%). These coordinates describe proximity to idealized reference shapes; they do not by themselves establish a reaction pathway or dynamic interconversion.
