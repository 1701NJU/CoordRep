# Figure 6 — multi-resolution identity and periodic organization

The current Figure 6 introduces the L0–L3 molecular identity hierarchy,
illustrates it with the five-record XEYVEC family, and reports the periodic
MID/SID organization of the public CSD MOF Collection.

- `Figure6_Multiresolution_Chemical_Insight_FINAL.svg` is the manuscript
  vector artwork.
- `Figure6_source_render.pdf` and `Figure6_preview_600dpi.png` are inspection
  renders.
- `Figure6_source_data.csv` contains the current 84,453-record molecular-tier
  family results and periodic aggregate values.
- `Figure6_caption.txt` gives the interpretation boundary: XEYVEC comprises
  static polymorph-associated intermediate basins, and MID/SID pairing is an
  association with crystallographic symmetry rather than a macroscopic
  chirality assignment.
