Figure 6. Multi-resolution coordination identity connects molecular families to orientation-sensitive organization in periodic frameworks. (A) CoordRep-ID defines four nested molecular comparison levels. L0 is the full canonical geometry-resolved record; L1 retains the binned-shape and relation signatures, oxidation state, CN, metal, and ligand identities; L2 retains the best-shape label, metal, CN, and ligand identities; and L3 retains metal, CN, and supported ligand connectivity. (B) In the corrected 84,453-record connectivity-supported CSD tier, 3,491 refcode families contain at least two records (9,056 records total). Mean within-family agreement is 11.2%, 83.5%, 96.6%, and 97.4% at L0–L3; complete concordance occurs for 356, 2,837, 3,351, and 3,379 families, respectively. (C) The XEYVEC Cu(II), CN = 4 family is the largest corrected same-L3 family that occupies more than one best-shape basin. Five independent determinations resolve two reproducible regions along the square-planar–tetrahedral continuum: three yellow C2/c records are Td-leaning and two green P-1 records are SP-leaning. All retain the same [CuCl4]2− coordination constitution and organize as 5→2→2→1 identities; the finite CShM values identify intermediate, polymorph-locked geometries rather than ideal SP and Td end points. (D) In the periodic CSD MOF atlas, 23,587 of 36,552 metric local-state identities (64.53%) split into exactly two orientation-sensitive stereo identities, yielding 60,139 stereo identities. At least one metric-equivalent/stereo-distinct local-state pair occurs in 7,721 of 9,189 state-bearing entries not flagged as Sohncke (84.02%) but in 0 of 1,759 Sohncke entries. Pairing was derived from the supplied P1 coordinate records before the separate original-space-group flag was joined. This association does not by itself assign a continuous chirality magnitude or macroscopic framework chirality.
