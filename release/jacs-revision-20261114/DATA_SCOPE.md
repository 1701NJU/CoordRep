# Distribution and interpretation scope

## Distributed

- Authors' Figure 4 artwork, preserved A/B raster and editable C/D vectors.
- Authors' flattened PyMOL validation illustrations, not scene/session files.
- Aggregate property source statistics already released with the saved models.
- Public case identifiers/refcodes and source publication DOIs.
- Authors' reference qualifications, comparison labels and aggregate counts.
- Molecular reference indices into the separately obtained public external XYZ
  inputs, without those coordinates; these are not native CSD atom mappings.
- Authors' implementation code, source hashes and constructed controls.

## Not distributed

- Raw CIF, RES, HKL, FCF or native CSD coordinate/structure files.
- Real XYZ/MOL/SDF coordinate files from any third-party dataset.
- Editable PyMOL PSE sessions or atom-vector/coordinate ledgers.
- Licensed native CSD atom-level mapping files.
- Third-party paper PDFs, user manuscript/review DOCX files or reviewer text.
- Credentials, license keys, internal service addresses or workstation paths.

Readers must obtain original sources from the cited providers under the
applicable terms. A public DOI or source identifier does not grant a right to
redistribute the provider's full structure or paper. CSD-dependent execution
requires an authorized local CSD installation; public aggregate tables and
constructed controls can be checked without CSD access.

## Endpoint definitions

Processing all 44 molecular inputs is distinct from the 21 complete-reference
donor-set tests. Keeping all 84 cases in the register is not 84 chemical
successes. Partial/scalar and other-state references remain labelled rather
than being silently promoted to complete optimized-state references.

The two NUXNIG Cu sites each retain the authors' four primary donors and two
additional longer contacts. They remain 0/2 exact primary-set matches, not
two independently established six-coordinate assignments. All six Au-Tl
neighbour assertions are directed local relations, not six distinct physical
bonds in a displayed fragment.

The 16 complete disorder-conditional local references contain 72 physical
donor images. They are not 16 crystals or a global disorder ensemble. Two
additional candidates for BF4 orientation 3 are retained outside the scored
denominator because a complete independent donor assignment is unavailable.
Deposited occupancies do not establish correlations between different sites.

The literature-referenced panel is development/regression validation, not
prospective human expert-blind testing. Native-source transcription fidelity,
chemical reference agreement and canonical-invariance controls are separate
measurements. No source policy or benchmark threshold was changed when
assembling this publication package.
