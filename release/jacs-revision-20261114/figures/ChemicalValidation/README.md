# Chemical validation illustrations

These authors' illustrations were rendered by CPU ray tracing in PyMOL 3.1.0.
They are selected views of the qualified comparisons in Tables S16-S18, not
84 independently validated structures. The deposited or published source
positions were not reoptimized for the illustrations. All panel annotations
use Arial. Source publications are listed in
[`../../chemical_validation/`](../../chemical_validation/).

## R6-1 Molecular examples

Panels A-F show original external Fe cases 01, 02, 04, 11, 30 and 43:
N-bound thiocyanates; amine/chloride donors; thioether/phosphine donors;
porphyrin N donors; macrocycle/acetonitrile/oxo donors; and five C-bound
carbonyl ligands. Local donor compositions are not complete molecular
formulas. Hydrogen atoms are omitted and bond orders are not assigned by
the displayed skeletal links.

## R6-2 Crystallographic examples

A: the Fe/Cp2 core in the dppf-containing Au compound TAXDOL; outer P/Au
substituents are omitted. B: the zirconium-oxalate unit in MOGFUL; both Zr
positions are shown, but only the original asymmetric-unit target enters
the one-target count. C: three NiO5F spheres sharing one F in GENCEL;
outer ligand backbone and K environment are omitted. D: Au-Tl relations
in TEGVAD; four distinct displayed physical links cover six directed local
neighbour assertions. Boundary images are not additional independent targets.
E/F: NUXNIG Cu1/Cu2, with the authors' CuN2O2 primary donor sets in solid
links and the two longer contacts at each Cu in dashed links. Both Cu
sites are counted as extra-contact differences, not exact primary-set matches.

## R6-3 Disorder alternatives

A/D: one of the six split Zn-site pairs in BOWCAV. B/E: Na1 under BF4
orientations 1/2 in MAXNUY01. C/F: Na2 under those orientations. Blue
outlines mark neighbouring-cell donor images. Mutually exclusive groups
are not combined. The full comparison is 12 Zn plus four Na local
alternatives, with 72 physical donor images. The two candidates from BF4
orientation 3 remain outside the complete-reference denominator and are
not shown here.

Only the flattened authors' PNG renderings are distributed. No editable
PyMOL scenes, native CSD structures or raw atomic coordinate files are included.
