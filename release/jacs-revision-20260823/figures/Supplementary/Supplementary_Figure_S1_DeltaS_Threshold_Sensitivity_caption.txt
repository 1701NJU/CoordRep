Supplementary Figure S1. Sensitivity of the near-boundary fraction to the ΔS threshold. Empirical cumulative fractions are shown for the same 33,863 corrected coordinate-space CShM records used in Figure 3 (CN = 4, N = 16,682; CN = 5, N = 6,356; CN = 6, N = 10,825). A record is classified as near-boundary when the unrounded margin S_second − S_best is strictly less than the indicated ΔS threshold. Curves were evaluated from ΔS = 0 to 3 in increments of 0.01. The dashed line marks the default ΔS = 1, at which 39/16,682 CN = 4 records (0.23%), 693/6,356 CN = 5 records (10.90%), and 294/10,825 CN = 6 records (2.72%) are classified as near-boundary; the corresponding overall fraction is 1,026/33,863 (3.03%). The curves are empirical and unsmoothed. ΔS measures numerical proximity to the implemented ideal-shape references and is not interpreted as evidence for a reaction pathway or dynamic interconversion.
