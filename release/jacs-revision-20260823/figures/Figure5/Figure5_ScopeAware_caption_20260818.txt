Figure 5. Chemistry-aware construction and release-wide CSD audit. (A) A Cu₂(μ-Cl)₂ core, Fe(η⁵-Cp)₂, and the XUMMID41 CuN₄Cl₂ first sphere illustrate typed record construction. In XUMMID41, four equatorial N donors and two axial Cl incidences surround Cu. The two Cl incidences arise from one wrapped chloride orbit but carry distinct lattice-translation labels, τ = (0, 0, 0) and τ = (0, 0, 1), thereby preserving the physical CN = 6 first sphere. Each in-domain d-block atom defines a metal-site node. A shared donor is stored once with multiple metal incidences. A 2–12-atom component induced by native π/delocalized metal–donor bonds and connected through the source donor graph becomes one π-fragment site with explicit membership, hapticity, and centroid-derived local-geometry summaries; larger components remain ambiguous candidates. For periodic records, the donor group retains a hashed wrapped-orbit witness and each incidence retains the relative integer translation τ; native lifted Cartesian coordinates provide distance and geometry. Disorder or missing geometry/translation changes the retained outcome rather than causing exclusion. (B) Of 1,371,757 April 2025 CSD entries, 602,116 contained a three-dimensional structure and an in-domain d-block center. All yielded schema-valid audit records and passed independent source-fidelity checks. The mutually exclusive partition is 253,522 strict nonpolymeric single-center, 233,622 nonpolymeric multiple-center/external-relation, and 114,972 polymeric entries. (C) At least one structural site is retained in 601,664 entries (99.9249%), every in-domain site is structural in 601,402 entries (99.8814%), and 1,984,062/1,986,198 metal sites are structural (99.8925%). Terminal entry outcomes are 293,850 resolved, 76,608 partial, 231,206 disorder-ambiguous, and 452 audit-only; a vertical mark locates the sub-print-width audit-only segment. (D) Donor groups with bridge degree > 1, confirmed haptic/π sites, and resolved nonzero-translation edges occur in 147,348, 102,241, and 94,452 entries, respectively (nonexclusive). Thirty-four entries contain an oversized candidate atom set; candidate objects do not trigger the haptic flag, although seven entries also contain a confirmed haptic site. The public CSD MOF pipeline processed 15,905/15,906 entries and emitted 172,332 local states from 10,948 entries; 74,354 states (43.15%) contain a nonzero translation edge. Of 114,972 full-CSD polymeric targets, 114,916 are translation-complete, nine retain unresolved periodic-edge evidence, and 47 are edge-less audit-only outcomes.
