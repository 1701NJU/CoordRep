Figure 2. Attachment-aware canonicalization removes input conventions while retaining coordination stereochemistry. (A) trans-[Co(en)2Cl2]+ provides a chemically recognizable example. Ligand payload, chelate attachment, and donor-class keys are refined with rotation-invariant metal–donor and donor–donor distances. Only symmetry-allowed residual ties are enumerated, and the lexicographically minimum complete CoordRep-State is retained. The a/b labels denote interchangeable ligand instances, not identity levels. (B) End-to-end test on a fixed graph-disjoint tmQMg/PBE cohort of 1,000 mononuclear CN = 4–6 snapshots, with K = 100 trials per structure in each of three arms: proper rigid motion, consistent atom/connection-table reindexing, and both. Cell values are the percentages of structures for which all 100 variants matched the reference exactly. CoordRep-State collapsed 1,000/1,000 structures in every arm and matched 300,000/300,000 variants with zero mismatches. Ordered Cartesian coordinates, ordered distance matrices, the declared relation-stripped key, and source-order serialization are controls rather than external models. (C) Among 6,427 unique graphs in the fixed screen, 5,173 contained a non-singleton legal ligand or donor-rank orbit after invariant refinement. A fixed graph-disjoint subset of 1,000 structures underwent 100 atom reindexings each. Invariant-key sorting without exact orbit enumeration collapsed 380/1,000 structures and matched 64,834/100,000 variants; full minimum-whole-record canonicalization collapsed 1,000/1,000 and matched 100,000/100,000. All full-exact strings passed grammar round-trip validation, candidate counts were stable, and no variant or fatal errors occurred. A non-automorphic adjacent/opposite attachment-set control remained distinct (zero false merges), whereas missing attachment identity failed closed. (D) Idealized octahedral fac- and mer-[Co(NH3)3Cl3] models isolate relation-level specificity. The two models use the same exact octahedral donor coordinates and share Co(III)/CN = 6, ligand payload, monodentate attachments, and the O_h geometry field. In the fac model, the three ammine N donors occupy one face and all three trans axes are N–Cl. In the mer model, two ammine N donors are trans, giving one N–N, one N–Cl, and one Cl–Cl trans axis. These inventories enter the rel field and yield distinct CoordRep-States without using the human-readable fac/mer labels as encoded inputs. The models are schematic rather than CSD records, and no experimental continuous-shape values are implied. The tmQMg/PBE experiments are fixed snapshot challenges rather than April 2025 CSD prevalence estimates. The tested attachment-aware implementation is frozen as CoordRep 1.1.2rc3 in release/jacs-revision-20260823.
