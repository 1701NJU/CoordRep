Figure 6. Canonical translation-resolved coordination across crystallographic boundaries and the CSD MOF Collection. (A) The XUMMID CuN4Cl2 first sphere contains four Cu-N contacts (2.062 Å) and two axial Cu-Cl contacts (2.696 and 2.768 Å) across the c = 1 unit-cell boundary. (B) The two Cu-Cl contacts arise from one wrapped chloride orbit but remain distinct through their relative translations; retaining both edges gives the CN 6 Oh state [S(Oh) = 1.8748], whereas merging them by wrapped donor orbit produces a false CN 5 SPY record [S(SPY) = 1.0100]. CShM values refer to different coordination-number reference sets and are therefore not compared as a common quality scale. (C) The canonical pipeline processed 15,905 of 15,906 CSD MOF Collection entries and emitted 172,332 local metal-site states from 10,948 entries; 74,354 states (43.15%) contain at least one nonzero translation-labelled edge, resolving 36,552 metric and 60,139 stereo-distinct identities. (D) Compound crystallographic re-expression of 400 family-disjoint entries (5,920 sites) left the entry-state multisets and local metric and stereo identities unchanged under simultaneous atom/donor permutation, origin translation, periodic-image and orbit relabeling, and orientation-preserving unimodular basis changes; metric identity was also retained in 50 of 50 tested orientation-reversing basis comparisons.
