# Figure-to-manuscript map

This map prevents the historical and current revision assets from being
interchanged.

| Main-text figure | Authoritative release files | What the files support |
|---|---|---|
| Figure 1 | `Figure1/` | Canonical, continuous, compositional record anatomy; molecular, haptic, multinuclear, and periodic first-sphere scope |
| Figure 2 | `Figure2/` | Exact canonicalization invariance and L0–L3 identity hierarchy |
| Figure 3 | `Figure3/` | CShM geometry atlases, JT proxy distributions, and PCA source tables |
| Figure 4 | `Figure4/` | Donor-relation and shape/CShM fields on graph-degenerate pairs; SchNet and ViSNet controls |
| Figure 5 | `Figure5/` | April-2025 CSD molecular survey and scope expansion |
| Figure 6 | `Figure6/` | Periodic quotient, translation-labelled edges, collection census, and family-disjoint invariance |

Vector files (`.svg`, `.pdf`) are the preferred artwork for manuscript
replacement. PNGs are preview files. Internal-licensed source tables are not
included; public source tables are explicitly named without the
`internal_licensed` suffix.

The current manuscript's Figure 2 family denominator is the frozen 765-family
cohort (2,112 records). Any old caption or figure referring to 886 families is
retired and should not be used in a resubmission package.
